### Where the data comes from

The path data is copied unchanged from [Archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify)
(`archify/renderers/shared/generated-brand-marks.mjs`), which generated it from
[Simple Icons 16.28.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.28.0).
The OpenAI mark is the exception: Archify took it from OpenAI's
[official brand assets](https://openai.com/brand/).

Simple Icons makes its collection available under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.28.0/LICENSE.md).

explains, CC0 for the collection does not mean every underlying icon is CC0. A
missing license entry below is not a grant of permission.
