How to read this: the line is the path a mouse move or key press takes, in order, from your hand to the screen. Each problem card hangs under the stop where that problem lives. A quiet grey stop has no card.

  1. Mouse and keyboardsense your hand
    Common

    “My mouse stops on my glass desk.”

    WhyThe sensor photographs the desk, and clear glass has no texture to photograph.

    Try firstUse a mouse pad.

    Normal

    “The first move after a break lags.”

    WhyThe mouse naps to save its battery. Your first move wakes it up.

    Try firstNothing: it is normal. If the lag never goes away, check the battery.

  2. Radiocarries each report
    Common

    “My arrow stutters or jumps.”

    WhyNoise or distance loses reports, and the mouse has to send them again.

    Try firstMove the receiver closer: a front USB 2 port or a short extension cable.
