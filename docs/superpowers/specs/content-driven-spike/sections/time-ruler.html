How to read this: time runs left to right in milliseconds (ms), and each bar is one stop of a key press. Top row: a fast receiver, checked 1,000 times a second. Bottom row: a basic one, checked 125 times a second. A solid bar is a fixed time; a striped bar is an “up to” or “varies” time.

  • solid: a fixed time
  • striped: "up to", or varies
  • orange: the report on the radio
  • amber: waiting

These are typical sizes. Real ones vary by device.

The radio is one of the shortest stops; waiting for the screen to redraw is usually the longest. A faster receiver shortens only one small bar.
